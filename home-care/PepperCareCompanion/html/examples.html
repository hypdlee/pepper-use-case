Short words work best

  • Hello, then your first name
  • Exercise · Breathing · Chat
  • Music: a song or the saxophone
  • Activities: dance, quiz or snakes and ladders. In the quiz say one, two or three. In the game say roll (or touch the dice)
  • Memories: Relive Malta, one picture and one question at a time
  • Medicine (check-in) · Reminder · “When is my medicine?”
  • Help alerts the care staff (shown on this tablet; Pepper does not page anyone)
  • Stop stops any movement or music
  • Next (or skip) and Again during exercises; each one lasts 40 seconds
  • Next patient when someone else would like a turn

Speak after Pepper has finished talking. Pepper helps one person at a time; others can add their name to the queue on this tablet.
