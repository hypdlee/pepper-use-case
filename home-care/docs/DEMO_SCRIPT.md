# Demo script (about 6 minutes)

Set up: Pepper standing, tablet showing the start screen, a second person ready to play the carer with the Staff view.

1. **Start (20 s).** Say "Hello". Pepper greets, says in one sentence what it can do (exercise and breathing, music and activities, chat, memories of Malta, medicine and reminders) and asks for a first name. Say the name, or type it on the tablet. The screen changes to "Hello, <name>." with eight tiles. After the check-in Pepper names the same eight topics (exercise, breathing, music, activities, chat, memories of Malta, medicine, reminder).
2. **Relive Malta (60 s).** Tap Relive Malta. Pepper says "Let's remember Malta together" and reads the first card while it is on screen, then stays quiet. Pepper asks one question with two answers, for example "Which pastizzi do you like best, ricotta or peas?". Tap an answer or say it: Pepper gives one short reply and waits. Talk about it as a carer would, tap I like this, then Next. Open Staff: the record shows "Memories", what was liked and "Said: Pastizzi and tea: Ricotta".
3. **Exercise (60 s).** Tap Exercise. Pepper's arms and the tablet figure move together, with a countdown. Say "next" to skip, then "stop".
4. **Breathing (30 s).** Tap Breathe. The circle grows and shrinks with Pepper's arms.
5. **Music (30 s).** Tap Music. Pepper asks "Which one?" and three songs and the Saxophone appear. Tap "Mama Liked the Roses" or say "song two". Say "stop".
6. **Activities and quiz (60 s).** Tap Activities. Pepper asks "Dance, a quiz, or snakes and ladders?" and the three choices appear on the tablet by themselves (try saying "activities" instead of tapping). Say "quiz" or tap the question mark. Pepper reads a question with three options. Wait until Pepper has finished the options, then answer one by voice ("two") and the next by touch. Pepper says whether it was right and gives the score after the fifth question. Tap Another quiz to show that the questions change.
7. **Help (40 s).** While an activity runs, tap Help. Everything stops, Pepper's eyes flash amber, and the banner names the person. The Staff button shows a red 1. Open Staff, press Seen.
8. **Needs and questions (40 s).** While an activity is on, say "I am thirsty". Pepper answers and tells the carer; the Staff banner says "would like a drink" and the activity carries on. Ask "What time is it?". When the activity is over, say "Tell me a joke". Say "I have fallen" to show the urgent case: everything stops and the alert is red. Say "Surprise me" to hear one suggestion that has not been done yet.
9. **Records (20 s).** In Staff, press Clear all records, then Yes. The list is empty and Pepper's memory holds nothing about the session.

If voice fails in a loud room, every step works by tapping. If the tablet page does not load, Pepper says so and keeps trying, and the whole session still works by voice.

Optional: start the laptop dashboard before the demo (see the README) and put the laptop screen next to the robot. When you say "I am thirsty" or press Help, the red alert card, with its reason, appears there within about a second.
